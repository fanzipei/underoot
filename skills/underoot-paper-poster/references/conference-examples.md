# Inspected poster examples — 2026-11-13

- [ICLR 2025 Self-Introspective Decoding (SID)](https://iclr.cc/media/iclr-2025/Slides/28166.pdf), one actual poster sheet despite the URL directory: complete identity and institutional marks at the top; methods combine source diagrams and examples; experimental sections retain real comparison tables and quantitative charts. Adopt the evidence variety and local figure/claim pairing, not its dense abstract or tiny text.
- [ICLR 2025 I-Con](https://iclr.cc/media/iclr-2025/Slides/29352.pdf), one sheet: a short top-level message and central loss/representation matrix establish the argument; the architecture and numerical classification comparison are distinct evidence areas; the QR is clearly separate. Adopt the unequal, purpose-driven allocation of canvas space. Do not imitate the source's branding or pretend a regular three-column template reproduces its bespoke composition.
- [ICML 2025 poster instructions](https://icml.cc/Conferences/2025/PosterInstructions): sizes are venue-dependent; underoot A0 presets are not a promise of current venue compliance.

The earlier renderer gave copied prose too much emphasis and could omit central result tables. The revised inventory and grouped-table portability address that omission. Whole-sheet quality still requires checking actual chart labels, comparison conditions and whether the most important evidence is sufficiently large. A filled border is not filled scientific content; additional panels are not automatically better. Record unresolved omissions rather than claiming all figures/tables are shown from source-ID coverage alone.
